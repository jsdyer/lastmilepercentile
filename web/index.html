What the band means

At the median (P50), roughly half the tests were slower and half were faster. The published cutoffs give a range for your result. Equal cutoffs can span several percentiles.

Who was measured?

M-Lab groups voluntary tests by month, estimated city and network number. This extract includes Canadian groups with at least 100 tests in the selected direction. Tests can come from the same connection more than once. City estimates, Wi-Fi and device conditions can affect the comparison.
